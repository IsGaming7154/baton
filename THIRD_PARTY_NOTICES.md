# Third-Party Notices

## Sintel

The bundled demo clip (`app/src/main/res/raw/sample_clip.mp4`) is an adaptation
of an excerpt of **Sintel**, from 01:41.208 to 03:09.792. It has been modified:

- The picture is re-encoded to 854x356.
- The film's English dialogue is replaced by a German dub. The soundtrack is the
  Blender Foundation's Sintel music-and-effects stem, which carries no voices,
  with German dialogue mixed over it, voiced by Amazon Polly (synthetic voices,
  Daniel and Vicki).
- The German dialogue text comes from the Blender Foundation's `sintel_de.srt`,
  adapted: lines 3, 4 and 11 are shortened, and "Torwaechter" is spelled
  "Torwächter".

The English subtitles (`app/src/main/res/raw/sample_clip_en.srt`) are the text
of the Blender Foundation's `sintel_en.srt`, retimed to the German dialogue.
